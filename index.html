50/50/90 COMPLETE SHOOTING

Learn It. Film Yourself. Train It. Measure It.

Complete Shooting is the basketball-development curriculum of The 50/50/90 Standard. It is a self-guided system built to develop repeatable shooting mechanics, purposeful practice habits, measurable progress, and confident game transfer.

The curriculum progresses through four phases across 12 weeks, moving from shooting foundation and repeatability to range, movement, pressure, and game-ready execution.

06RE-TEST & MEASURE PROGRESS
07TRANSFER IT TO THE GAME

THE COMPLETE SHOOTER

Mechanics. Footwork. Range. Repetition. Movement. Decision-making. Confidence. Pressure. Transfer.

PHASE I

Foundation

Establish repeatable form, balance, preparation, footwork, shooting rhythm, and disciplined practice habits.

PHASE II

Range & Repeatability

Extend effective shooting range while maintaining balance, direction, rhythm, and repeatable mechanics.

PHASE III

Movement & Game Preparation

Develop catch-and-shoot footwork, relocation, pull-ups, movement shooting, and faster shot preparation.

PHASE IV

Pressure & Transfer

Challenge the shot under competition, fatigue, time, decision-making, and game-like conditions.

WHAT THE CURRICULUM INCLUDES

12-week progression • six-day weekly training structure • 30+ shooting drills • baseline and re-test protocols • weekly progress tests • shooting journal • mental-performance training • pressure work • game-transfer development
